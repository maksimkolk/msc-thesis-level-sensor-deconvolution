# MSc Thesis - Level Sensor Deconvolution

This repository contains my MSc thesis in Industrial and Applied Mathematics at Eindhoven University of Technology, completed in collaboration with ASML.

The research focuses on an inverse problem involving the reconstruction of wafer topography from noisy and spatially filtered level-sensor measurements. The work includes mathematical modelling, numerical estimation, synthetic validation, and robustness analysis.

**Thesis:** *Level Sensor Deconvolution*  
**Degree:** MSc Industrial and Applied Mathematics  
**University:** Eindhoven University of Technology  
**Year:** 2026
